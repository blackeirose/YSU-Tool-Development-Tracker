Design review focus

What should be resolved before design advances

PRIORITY 1
Confirm the governing apparatus schedule and maximum test flow.

The Model 34 reference and the 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000 GPM calculation basis are not internally aligned. Geometry changes should follow this decision, not precede it.

PRIORITY 2
Correct usable-volume calculations.

The gross 31 ft water prism counts the two concrete baffles as water. The current 8 ft condition is approximately 19,363 gal before optional sump volume.

HIGH
Define suction configuration and test instrumentation.

Confirm simultaneous hose count/size, manufacturer-based suction losses, vortex control, and the method used to measure and document actual test flow.

STUDY
Reframe de-aeration around residence time and return-water behavior.

Baffle opening velocity alone cannot prove complete de-aeration. Evaluate stagger, jet dissipation, above-water air entrainment and suction separation.

COORD.
Complete operational and structural coordination.

Water-level indication, make-up, overflow, drain-down, backflow protection, maintenance access, liquid-tight concrete, buoyancy and vehicle loading should be confirmed in the applicable disciplines.
